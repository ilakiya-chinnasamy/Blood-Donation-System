BMI Information

BMI is a calculation based on height and weight. For adults, general reference categories are below 18.5 (underweight), 18.5–24.9 (healthy-weight range), 25–29.9 (overweight), and 30 or above (obesity range).

Important: These categories do not decide blood donation eligibility. Blood banks assess weight, haemoglobin, health history, and other donation criteria.
